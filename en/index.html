Three pillars for ethical AI

Bias

We aim to make bias in AI visible, with attention to Danish language, culture and social context.

Responsible AI

We focus on transparency, human judgement and accountability in the development and use of AI.

Sustainability

We aim to promote insight into AI’s energy and resource use, and the balance between benefits and impact.

In preparation

A Danish perspective on AI bias

DkBBQ is the planned Danish benchmark project for examining bias in language models. Its starting point is localisation: examples should reflect Danish language and social context.

No DkBBQ results or datasets have been published on this website yet.
