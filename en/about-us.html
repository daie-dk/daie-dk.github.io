About the association

Dansk AI-Etikforening focuses on ethical questions around artificial intelligence in Denmark. We aim to make complex questions accessible and contribute to a better-informed conversation about AI.

Independent insight

Space for critical questions about how AI works and its consequences.

Local context

Attention to Danish language, culture and social context.

Sharing knowledge

Making research and practical insights understandable and accessible.

A shared commitment to AI ethics

The Dutch, Danish and Norwegian organisations share an interest in local AI evaluation. Each organisation develops its own activities and publications.
