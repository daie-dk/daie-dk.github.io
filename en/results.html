DkBBQIn preparation
In preparation

A Danish perspective on AI bias

DkBBQ is the planned Danish benchmark project for examining bias in language models. Its starting point is localisation: examples should reflect Danish language and social context.

No DkBBQ results or datasets have been published on this website yet.

The Norwegian sister organisation’s NoBBQ results are not presented as Danish results. DkBBQ will have its own publications, methodology and context.
